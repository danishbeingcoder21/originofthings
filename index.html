About This Academic Project

Origin of Things is a dedicated university student project founded on a simple question: How did ordinary objects and fundamental phenomena come into existence?

Born in a college dorm out of late-night curiosities and academic inquiry, this project aims to bridge the gap between heavy academic encyclopedias and everyday readers. It brings stories of human resilience, serendipity, and celestial evolution into one immersive haven designed to rekindle the joy of learning.
