Welcome to the Journey of Existence

From the vastness of the universe to the screen you are looking at right now, everything has a beginning. Let's explore!

1. The Universe

Born from the Big Bang 13.8 billion years ago, creating space, time, and matter.

2. The Earth

Formed 4.5 billion years ago from a cloud of dust and gas around the young Sun.

3. Water

Brought to Earth by icy comets and asteroids during the Late Heavy Bombardment.

4. Life

Began in the deep oceans around hydrothermal vents nearly 3.5 billion years ago.

5. Dinosaurs

Ruled the Earth for 165 million years before a massive asteroid hit.

6. Humanity

Homo sapiens evolved in Africa about 300,000 years ago and spread across the globe.

7. Fire

First controlled by our early ancestors around 1 million years ago, changing our diet and survival.

8. The Wheel

Invented around 3500 BC in Mesopotamia, originally used for making pottery, not transportation!

9. Electricity

Discovered over centuries, but harnessed for human use in the late 19th century.

10. The Internet

Created in the 1960s as ARPANET, connecting humanity in ways never imagined before.
